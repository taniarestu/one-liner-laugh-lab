Entry for the [make.fun bounty "One-Liner Laugh Lab"](https://make.fun/c/u-suroso-69-one-liner-laugh-lab-6ccf3aae) ($2.50, closes 26 Nov 2026).

## The entry

> I did not eat your sock. I merely relocated it to a secure facility under the couch, where it will be processed indefinitely.

One line, 125 characters: the excuse a robot vacuum gives after eating a
sock, deadpan, original, and tight.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only, no network) writes the line to
`/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
